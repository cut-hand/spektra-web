The six SpektraLab product profiles (four B&W films, one paper and the
CineStill alias) retain their own metadata and source notices. Their provenance
is documented in licenses/PRODUCT-PROFILES-NOTICE.md. They are not attributed
to Andrea Volpato. CineStill resolves the unmodified Vision3 500T base profile.
SpektraLab's README declares film/paper profiles and print LUTs CC BY-SA 4.0.

This browser implementation is an independent experimental adaptation of the
published process. It adds a three-basis spectral reconstruction, analytic
colour-matching functions, browser scanning and grading approximations.
It does not reproduce the native Hanatos/DIR/CAM16 pipeline or claim numerical
parity with SpektraLab or spektrafilm.

Exported .cube files are newly sampled direct derivatives of the selected
material profiles and colour settings. They are licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 and
carry creator credit, source links, licence links and modification notices
in their headers. Spatial effects, grain, edges and date stamps are excluded.
The JavaScript implementation is GPL-3.0-or-later; see LICENSE and NOTICE.html.
